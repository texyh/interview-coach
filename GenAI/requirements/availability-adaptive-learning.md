# 24/7 Availability and Adaptive Learning

## Feature Description
The system shall provide continuous availability and adapt dynamically to user needs and progress.

## Functional Requirements

### FR-029: Continuous Availability
- The system shall be accessible 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
- The system shall support multiple concurrent users without performance degradation
- The system shall maintain high uptime (99.9% availability)

### FR-030: On-Demand Access
- The system shall allow users to start practice sessions instantly
- The system shall support quick session types for time-constrained users
- The system shall enable pause and resume functionality for sessions

### FR-031: Adaptive Learning Engine
- The system shall adjust difficulty based on user performance
- The system shall modify preparation focus areas based on progress
- The system shall personalize content recommendations dynamically

### FR-032: Performance Optimization
- The system shall maintain fast response times during peak usage
- The system shall scale resources automatically based on demand
- The system shall optimize content delivery for different devices and connection speeds

## Acceptance Criteria
- System maintains 99.9% uptime with maximum 4 hours downtime per year
- Response times remain under 3 seconds during peak hours
- Adaptive algorithms improve user outcomes by at least 20%
- Sessions can be accessed from any device with internet connectivity
