# Mock Interviews

## Feature Description
The system shall provide realistic interview simulation with AI-powered questioning and interaction.

## Functional Requirements

### FR-005: Interview Simulation
- The system shall conduct mock interviews with realistic questioning patterns
- The system shall ask both behavioral and technical questions appropriate to the role
- The system shall maintain conversational flow and follow-up questioning

### FR-006: Role-Specific Content
- The system shall adapt interview questions based on selected job role and industry
- The system shall include industry-specific scenarios and case studies
- The system shall cover different interview formats (behavioral, technical, case study, etc.)

### FR-007: Voice and Video Integration
- The system shall support voice-based interview sessions
- The system shall support video-based interview sessions with webcam integration
- The system shall simulate realistic interview environments

### FR-008: Question Variety
- The system shall include common interview questions across all industries
- The system shall provide technical questions for engineering roles
- The system shall include behavioral questions using STAR method framework

## Acceptance Criteria
- Mock interviews can be conducted in text, voice, or video format
- The system supports at least 5 different interview types per industry
- Voice recognition accuracy is above 95%
- Video sessions support standard webcam resolutions
