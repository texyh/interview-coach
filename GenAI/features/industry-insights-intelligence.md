# Industry Insights and Intelligence

## Feature Description

The system shall provide targeted insights about companies, industries, and current trends to enhance interview preparation.

## Functional Requirements

### FR-021: Company Research

- The system shall provide company-specific interview insights and formats
- The system shall include information about company culture and values
- The system shall aggregate recent company news and developments

### FR-022: Industry Analysis

- The system shall track and report on industry trends and developments
- The system shall identify key knowledge areas for specialized roles
- The system shall provide market insights relevant to job seekers

### FR-023: Interview Intelligence

- The system shall collect and analyze common interview questions by company
- The system shall track interview success rates and feedback patterns
- The system shall provide insider tips and preparation strategies

### FR-024: Content Updates

- The system shall regularly update company and industry information
- The system shall monitor news sources for relevant updates
- The system shall validate information accuracy and relevance

## Acceptance Criteria

- Company database covers Fortune 500 companies and major employers
- Industry insights are updated at least monthly
- Information accuracy is verified through multiple sources
- Users can access relevant insights for their target companies
