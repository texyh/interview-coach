# Question Bank Management

## Feature Description

The system shall maintain and manage an extensive database of interview questions across industries and roles.

## Functional Requirements

### FR-013: Question Database

- The system shall maintain a database of common interview questions
- The system shall include industry-specific and role-specific questions
- The system shall categorize questions by type (behavioral, technical, situational, case study)

### FR-014: Question Categorization

- The system shall organize questions by difficulty level (entry, mid, senior)
- The system shall tag questions by competency areas and skills
- The system shall maintain question relevance ratings

### FR-015: Randomized Practice

- The system shall provide randomized question selection for practice sessions
- The system shall allow users to focus on specific question categories
- The system shall avoid repeating recently practiced questions

### FR-016: Question Management

- The system shall allow administrators to add, edit, and remove questions
- The system shall track question performance metrics and user ratings
- The system shall regularly update question database with new content

## Acceptance Criteria

- Database contains at least 1000 questions across major industries
- Questions are properly categorized and tagged
- Random selection avoids duplicates within 50 questions
- Question difficulty matches user's experience level
