# Personalized Preparation Plan

## Feature Description

The system shall create customized interview preparation plans based on user profile analysis.

## Functional Requirements

### FR-001: Resume Analysis

- The system shall analyze user's uploaded resume to extract skills, experience, and qualifications
- The system shall identify the user's career level (entry, mid, senior, executive)
- The system shall extract relevant technical skills and domain expertise

### FR-002: Job Target Assessment

- The system shall allow users to specify target job roles and industries
- The system shall match job requirements with user's current skill set
- The system shall identify skill gaps and areas for improvement

### FR-003: Customized Learning Path

- The system shall generate a personalized preparation timeline based on interview date
- The system shall prioritize preparation areas based on job requirements and user weaknesses
- The system shall adapt the plan for different industries (software engineering, product management, marketing, etc.)

### FR-004: Skill Assessment

- The system shall conduct initial skill assessments through quizzes and practice questions
- The system shall evaluate user's strengths and weaknesses across different competency areas
- The system shall provide skill level ratings and improvement recommendations

## Acceptance Criteria

- Users can upload their resume and receive automated analysis within 30 seconds
- The system generates a preparation plan within 5 minutes of profile completion
- Plans are customized for at least 10 different industry categories
- Skill assessments cover both technical and soft skills relevant to the target role
