# Instant Feedback System

## Feature Description

The system shall provide immediate, detailed feedback on interview performance across multiple dimensions.

## Functional Requirements

### FR-009: Performance Analysis

- The system shall analyze response quality, completeness, and relevance
- The system shall evaluate use of specific examples and concrete details
- The system shall assess clarity and structure of communication

### FR-010: Speech Analysis

- The system shall analyze tone, pace, and speaking volume
- The system shall detect and count filler words (um, ah, like, etc.)
- The system shall evaluate speaking confidence and enthusiasm

### FR-011: AI Scoring System

- The system shall provide numerical scores for different competency areas
- The system shall generate overall interview readiness ratings
- The system shall track score improvements over time

### FR-012: Detailed Feedback Reports

- The system shall generate comprehensive feedback reports after each session
- The system shall provide specific improvement recommendations
- The system shall highlight strengths and areas for development

## Acceptance Criteria

- Feedback is generated within 30 seconds of interview completion
- Scoring system uses consistent criteria across all interviews
- Speech analysis works with different accents and speaking styles
- Feedback reports include actionable improvement suggestions
