# Emotional and Mental Support System

## Feature Description
The system shall provide comprehensive emotional support and mental preparation tools to help users manage interview anxiety and build confidence.

## Functional Requirements

### FR-037: Confidence Building
- The system shall provide motivational content and positive reinforcement
- The system shall offer confidence-building exercises and affirmations
- The system shall track and celebrate user achievements and improvements

### FR-038: Anxiety Management
- The system shall provide techniques for managing interview nerves and anxiety
- The system shall offer guidance on staying calm under pressure
- The system shall include stress reduction strategies and coping mechanisms

### FR-039: Mindfulness and Relaxation
- The system shall provide guided breathing exercises and relaxation techniques
- The system shall offer short mindfulness practices suitable for pre-interview preparation
- The system shall include progressive muscle relaxation and visualization exercises

### FR-040: Mental Preparation Tools
- The system shall provide mental rehearsal techniques and visualization exercises
- The system shall offer positive self-talk strategies and mindset coaching
- The system shall include techniques for reframing negative thoughts and building resilience

## Acceptance Criteria
- Support tools are evidence-based and psychologically sound
- Exercises are accessible in various formats (audio, text, video)
- Content addresses common interview-related anxiety triggers
- Tools can be used independently or integrated with practice sessions
