# Interactive Learning Resources

## Feature Description
The system shall provide comprehensive learning materials and frameworks to enhance interview preparation.

## Functional Requirements

### FR-017: Answer Frameworks
- The system shall provide STAR method framework for behavioral questions
- The system shall offer structured approaches for technical problem solving
- The system shall include frameworks for different question types

### FR-018: Educational Modules
- The system shall offer modules on salary negotiation techniques
- The system shall provide guidance on handling difficult questions
- The system shall include modules on project presentation skills

### FR-019: Resource Curation
- The system shall curate relevant video tutorials from industry experts
- The system shall provide links to additional reading materials
- The system shall recommend resources based on user's preparation needs

### FR-020: Interactive Content
- The system shall provide interactive exercises and practice scenarios
- The system shall include step-by-step guides for common interview challenges
- The system shall offer downloadable templates and checklists

## Acceptance Criteria
- Learning modules cover all major interview skill areas
- Resources are regularly updated and verified for accuracy
- Interactive content engages users and reinforces learning
- Materials are accessible in multiple formats (text, video, audio)
