# Networking and Follow-up Guidance

## Feature Description
The system shall provide comprehensive guidance on post-interview activities and professional networking.

## Functional Requirements

### FR-033: Follow-up Communication
- The system shall provide templates for thank-you emails after interviews
- The system shall guide users on appropriate follow-up timing and frequency
- The system shall offer advice on maintaining professional connections

### FR-034: Networking Strategies
- The system shall provide guidance on building professional networks
- The system shall suggest networking opportunities and events
- The system shall offer tips on LinkedIn optimization and professional branding

### FR-035: Portfolio and Resume Enhancement
- The system shall analyze and provide feedback on user portfolios
- The system shall suggest resume improvements based on target roles
- The system shall recommend ways to showcase achievements and projects

### FR-036: Career Development Advice
- The system shall provide long-term career planning guidance
- The system shall suggest skill development opportunities
- The system shall offer insights on career advancement strategies

## Acceptance Criteria
- Follow-up templates are customizable and professional
- Networking advice is current and industry-appropriate
- Portfolio feedback includes specific, actionable recommendations
- Career guidance aligns with user's goals and market trends
