# Progress Tracking and Analytics

## Feature Description
The system shall track user progress over time and provide detailed analytics on interview preparation improvement.

## Functional Requirements

### FR-025: Performance Tracking
- The system shall track improvements in response quality over time
- The system shall monitor confidence levels and interview readiness scores
- The system shall record completion of preparation activities and milestones

### FR-026: Goal Management
- The system shall allow users to set specific preparation goals
- The system shall provide reminders and notifications for goal deadlines
- The system shall track goal completion rates and celebrate achievements

### FR-027: Analytics Dashboard
- The system shall provide visual dashboards showing progress metrics
- The system shall display trends in performance across different skill areas
- The system shall show comparison with other users (anonymized benchmarking)

### FR-028: Progress Reports
- The system shall generate detailed progress reports for users
- The system shall provide insights on strengths and improvement areas
- The system shall recommend next steps based on current progress

## Acceptance Criteria
- Progress data is updated in real-time after each session
- Goals can be customized based on user's timeline and priorities
- Analytics provide actionable insights for continued improvement
- Reports are exportable and shareable with career coaches
